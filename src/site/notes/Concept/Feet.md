## Introduction

The words **"feet"** and **"foot"** run through the whole of Scripture — from the ground Moses stood on to the feet of Him who brings good news, to the enemies made a footstool. The Bible uses these words *hundreds* of times, so what follows **gathers the passages by their sense**: where you stand, how you walk, who you fall before, and what is placed beneath your feet.

Quotations follow the NASB. Read each in its own context before leaning on the note.

---

## A · Holy ground — sandals off the feet
*Where you stand can be holy. The feet are bared before it.*

> **Exodus 3:5** — "Then He said, 'Do not come near here; remove your sandals from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.'"

> **Joshua 5:15** — "The captain of the Lord's army said to Joshua, 'Remove your sandals from your feet, for the place where you are standing is holy.' And Joshua did so."

> **Acts 7:33** — "But the Lord said to him, 'Take off the sandals from your feet, for the place on which you are standing is holy ground.'"

---

## B · Washing the feet — hospitality and welcome
*The traveler's feet were washed at the door. To wash them was to receive the guest.*

> **Genesis 18:4** — "Please let a little water be brought and wash your feet, and rest yourselves under the tree."

> **Genesis 19:2** — "'…wash your feet; and you may rise early and go on your way.' They said however, 'No, but we shall spend the night in the square.'"

> **Genesis 24:32** — "…and water to wash his feet and the feet of the men who were with him."

> **Genesis 43:24** — "…and he gave them water, and they washed their feet; and he gave their donkeys fodder."

> **Judges 19:21** — "…so they washed their feet and ate and drank."

> **1 Samuel 25:41** — "…'Behold, your maidservant is a maid to wash the feet of my lord's servants.'"

> **Song of Solomon 5:3** — "I have washed my feet, how can I dirty them again?"

> **1 Timothy 5:10** — "…if she has washed the saints' feet, if she has assisted those in distress…"

## C · Jesus washes the disciples' feet
*The Lord takes the servant's basin. The greatest stoops lowest.* See [[Concept/Faith\|Faith]].

> **John 13:6** — "So He came to Simon Peter. He said to Him, 'Lord, do You wash my feet?'"

> **John 13:8** — "Peter said to Him, 'Never shall You wash my feet!' Jesus answered him, 'If I do not wash you, you have no part with Me.'"

> **John 13:9** — "Simon Peter said to Him, 'Lord, then wash not only my feet, but also my hands and my head.'"

> **John 13:10** — "…'He who has bathed needs only to wash his feet, but is completely clean; and you are clean, but not all of you.'"

> **John 13:12–14** — "…'Do you know what I have done to you? …If I then, the Lord and the Teacher, washed your feet, you also ought to wash one another's feet.'"

*Feet become the place of worship, repentance, and discipleship.*

> **Luke 7:38** — "…standing behind Him at His feet, weeping, she began to wet His feet with her tears, and kept wiping them with the hair of her head, and kissing His feet and anointing them with the perfume."

> **Luke 7:44–46** — "…'you gave Me no water for My feet, but she has wet My feet with her tears… she has not ceased to kiss My feet… she anointed My feet with perfume.'"

> **Luke 10:39** — "She had a sister called Mary, who was seated at the Lord's feet, listening to His word."

> **John 11:2** — "It was the Mary who anointed the Lord with ointment, and wiped His feet with her hair…"

> **John 12:3** — "Mary then took a pound of very costly perfume… and anointed the feet of Jesus and wiped His feet with her hair; and the house was filled with the fragrance of the perfume."

> **Luke 8:35** — "…and found the man from whom the demons had gone out, sitting down at the feet of Jesus, clothed and in his right mind."

> **Acts 22:3** — "…educated under Gamaliel, strictly according to the law of our fathers…" *(KJV: "brought up… at the feet of Gamaliel").*

## E · Falling and bowing at the feet
*The feet are where the desperate and the reverent come down.*

> **1 Samuel 25:24** — "She fell at his feet and said, 'On me alone, my lord, be the blame.'"

> **2 Kings 4:37** — "Then she went in and fell at his feet and bowed herself to the ground…"

> **Esther 8:3** — "Then Esther spoke again to the king, fell at his feet, wept and implored him…"

> **Matthew 15:30** — "…and they laid them down at His feet; and He healed them."

> **Mark 5:22** — "…seeing Him, he fell at His feet…" *(Jairus).*

> **Mark 7:25** — "…a woman whose little daughter had an unclean spirit… came and fell at His feet."

> **Luke 17:16** — "…and he fell on his face at His feet, giving thanks to Him. And he was a Samaritan."

> **John 11:32** — "…Mary… fell at His feet, saying to Him, 'Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died.'"

> **Matthew 28:9** — "…And they came up and took hold of His feet and worshiped Him."

> **Acts 10:25** — "…Cornelius met him, and fell at his feet and worshiped him." *(Peter refuses it.)*

> **Revelation 1:17** — "When I saw Him, I fell at His feet like a dead man."

> **Revelation 3:9** — "…I will make them come and bow down at your feet, and make them know that I have loved you."

> **Revelation 19:10 / 22:8** — "Then I fell at his feet to worship him. But he said to me, 'Do not do that… worship God.'"

*Contrast — laying goods at the apostles' feet:*

> **Acts 4:35** — "…and lay them down at the apostles' feet, and they would be distributed to each as any had need."

> **Acts 4:37 / 5:2** — "…and brought the money and laid it at the apostles' feet." *(Barnabas; then Ananias, holding back part.)*

> **Romans 10:15** — "…just as it is written, 'How beautiful are the feet of those who bring good news of good things!'"

Compare the call of the messenger in [[Topic/Prepare the Way\|Prepare the Way]].

## H · Feet caught — snares, nets, and traps
*The wicked lay a net; the foot is what it catches.*

> **Job 13:27** — "You put my feet in the stocks and watch all my paths…"

> **Job 18:8** — "For he is thrown into the net by his own feet, and he steps on the webbing."

> **Psalm 9:15** — "…in the net which they hid, their own foot has been caught."

> **Psalm 25:15** — "My eyes are continually toward the Lord, for He will pluck my feet out of the net."

> **Proverbs 29:5** — "A man who flatters his neighbor is spreading a net for his steps."

> **Lamentations 1:13** — "…He has spread a net for my feet; He has turned me back…"

*And feet set free:*

> **Psalm 31:8** — "…You have set my feet in a large place."

> **Psalm 56:13** — "…that I may walk before God in the light of the living."

## I · Under the feet — dominion and the footstool
*What is placed beneath the feet is conquered. This is the throne-language of the Messiah.*

> **Psalm 8:6** — "You make him to rule over the works of Your hands; You have put all things under his feet."

> **Psalm 110:1** — "The Lord says to my Lord: 'Sit at My right hand until I make Your enemies a footstool for Your feet.'" *(Quoted in Matthew 22:44; Mark 12:36; Luke 20:43; Acts 2:35; Hebrews 1:13; 10:13.)*

> **Psalm 47:3** — "He subdues peoples under us and nations under our feet."

> **Joshua 10:24** — "…'Come near, put your feet on the necks of these kings.' So they came near and put their feet on their necks."

> **Malachi 4:3** — "You will tread down the wicked, for they will be ashes under the soles of your feet…"

> **1 Corinthians 15:25–27** — "For He must reign until He has put all His enemies under His feet… For He has put all things in subjection under His feet."

> **Ephesians 1:22** — "And He put all things in subjection under His feet, and gave Him as head over all things to the church…"

> **Hebrews 2:8** — "You have put all things in subjection under his feet…"

> **Romans 16:20** — "The God of peace will soon crush Satan under your feet."

*The earth itself as God's footstool:*

> **Isaiah 66:1** — "…'Heaven is My throne and the earth is My footstool.'"

> **Matthew 5:35** — "…or by the earth, for it is the footstool of His feet…"

> **Isaiah 60:13** — "…and I shall make the place of My feet glorious."

> **Ezekiel 43:7** — "…'this is the place of My throne and the place of the soles of My feet…'"

*When God draws near, the feet are the nearest and most terrible glory.*

> **Exodus 24:10** — "…and under His feet there appeared to be a pavement of sapphire, as clear as the sky itself."

> **2 Samuel 22:10 / Psalm 18:9** — "He bowed the heavens also, and came down with thick darkness under His feet."

> **Nahum 1:3** — "…in whirlwind and storm is His way, and clouds are the dust beneath His feet."

> **Habakkuk 3:5** — "Before Him goes pestilence, and plague comes after Him." *(v.5 in the KJV: "burning coals went forth at his feet.")*

> **Zechariah 14:4** — "In that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives… and the Mount of Olives will be split in its middle…"

> **Daniel 10:6** — "…His arms and feet like the gleam of polished bronze…"

> **Revelation 1:15** — "His feet were like burnished bronze, when it has been made to glow in a furnace…"

> **Revelation 2:18** — "…the Son of God, who has eyes like a flame of fire, and His feet are like burnished bronze."

> **Revelation 10:1–2** — "…his feet like pillars of fire… he placed his right foot on the sea and his left on the land."

> **Acts 13:51** — "But they shook off the dust of their feet in protest against them and went to Iconium."

*The feet are covered for the journey and for the battle.*

> **Exodus 12:11** — "…with your sandals on your feet, and your staff in your hand; and you shall eat it in haste—it is the Lord's Passover."

> **Deuteronomy 29:5** — "…your sandal has not worn out on your foot."

> **Ephesians 6:15** — "…and having shod your feet with the preparation of the gospel of peace."

## M · The priests' feet — the laver and the crossing
*At the tabernacle and at the Jordan, the feet are washed and the feet lead.*

> **Exodus 30:19–21** — "Aaron and his sons shall wash their hands and their feet from it… so that they will not die."

> **Exodus 40:31** — "…Moses and Aaron and his sons washed their hands and their feet from it."

> **Joshua 3:13** — "…as soon as the soles of the feet of the priests who carry the ark of the Lord… rest in the waters of the Jordan, the waters… will be cut off."

> **Joshua 3:15** — "…and the feet of the priests carrying the ark were dipped in the edge of the water…"

> **Joshua 4:18** — "…and the soles of the priests' feet were lifted up to the dry ground, the waters of the Jordan returned to their place…"

> **Deuteronomy 11:24 / Joshua 1:3** — "Every place on which the sole of your foot treads shall be yours…"

## O · Treading and trampling — judgment
*The same feet that carry good news also tread the winepress of wrath.*

> **Isaiah 26:6** — "The foot will trample it, the feet of the afflicted, the steps of the helpless."

> **Isaiah 63:3** — "I have trodden the wine trough alone, and from the peoples there was no man with Me. I also trod them in My anger…"

> **Daniel 7:7** — "…it was different from all the beasts that were before it, and it had ten horns… and it trampled down the remainder with its feet."

> **Micah 5:5** — "…when he treads in our palaces, then we will raise against him seven shepherds…"

> **Matthew 7:6** — "…they will trample them under their feet, and turn and tear you to pieces."

> **Revelation 14:20** — "And the wine press was trodden outside the city…"

## P · Feet of iron and clay — the kingdoms of men
*Nebuchadnezzar's statue: the empire that cannot stand on its own footing.*

> **Daniel 2:33** — "…its legs of iron, its feet partly of iron and partly of clay."

> **Daniel 2:34** — "…a stone was cut out without hands, and it struck the statue on its feet of iron and clay, and crushed them."

> **Daniel 2:41–42** — "…the feet and toes, partly of potter's clay and partly of iron… so the kingdom will be partly strong and partly brittle."

---

## Q · Idols — feet that cannot walk
*The dead god has feet and goes nowhere; those who make them become like them.*

> **Psalm 115:7** — "…they have feet, but they cannot walk; they cannot make a sound with their throat."

---

## R · The risen and glorified feet
*The proof of the resurrection, and the marks that remain.*

> **Luke 24:39–40** — "'See My hands and My feet, that it is I Myself…' And when He had said this, He showed them His hands and His feet."

> **John 20:12** — "…she saw two angels in white sitting, one at the head and one at the feet, where the body of Jesus had been lying."

> **John 11:44** — "…the man who had died came forth, bound hand and foot with wrappings…" *(Lazarus).*
