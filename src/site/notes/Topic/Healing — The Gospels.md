# Healing — The Gospels

> [!note] An ongoing meditation
> I have published this, but it is not finished. This is an ongoing, running meditation on healing through the different parts of the Bible. This part is the Gospels, where I go through and meditate on each portion, one healing at a time, and add what I receive as I go.

Every healing in Matthew, Mark, Luke and John, one a day. To meditate is to pray over it, understand it, and see myself doing it — for Christ has given me the ability to. Scripture is NASB throughout.

## 1 · Nobleman's Son at Cana

> Jesus said to him, "Go; your son is alive." The man believed the word that Jesus spoke to him and went _home_. — John 4:50

**Context.** Jesus has come back to Cana of Galilee, where He made the water wine (John 4:46). A royal official from Capernaum, whose son is at the point of death, comes to Him and begs Him to come down and heal his son. Jesus answers, "Unless you _people_ see signs and wonders, you _simply_ will not believe" (John 4:48), and the man presses, "Sir, come down before my child dies" (John 4:49). Jesus does not go. He sends only a word, and the man believes the word before he sees anything. On the road home his servants meet him: the fever left the boy at the seventh hour, the very hour Jesus said, Your son is alive. He and his whole household believe. John counts this as the second sign Jesus performed in Galilee (John 4:54).

**Witnesses:** John 4:46–54

And as he believed, his son was indeed alive.

## 2 · Demoniac in the Capernaum Synagogue

> And Jesus rebuked him, saying, "Be quiet, and come out of him!" — Mark 1:25

**Context.** It is the Sabbath in Capernaum, and Jesus is teaching in the synagogue. The people are amazed, "for He was teaching them as _one_ having authority, and not as the scribes" (Mark 1:22). A man with an unclean spirit cries out, "I know who You are: the Holy One of God!" (Mark 1:24). Jesus does not accept the testimony of a demon. He silences it and commands it out. The spirit throws the man into convulsions and comes out, and Luke adds that it came out "without doing him any harm" (Luke 4:35). The people ask, "What is this? A new teaching with authority! He commands even the unclean spirits, and they obey Him" (Mark 1:27). His fame spreads through all Galilee.

## 4 · The Leper Cleansed

> Jesus reached out with His hand and touched him, saying, "I am willing; be cleansed." And immediately his leprosy was cleansed. — Matthew 8:3

**Context.** Jesus has just come down from the mountain after the Sermon on the Mount (Matthew 8:1). Luke says the man was "covered with leprosy" (Luke 5:12). Under the Law a leper called out, "Unclean! Unclean!", lived alone, outside the camp (Leviticus 13:45–46), and whoever touched him became unclean. The leper does not doubt the power; he doubts the will: "Lord, if You are willing, You can make me clean" (Matthew 8:2). Jesus, "moved with compassion" (Mark 1:41), touches him before He speaks, and the uncleanness does not pass to Jesus; the cleanness passes to the leper. He sends him to the priest with the offering Moses commanded (Leviticus 14), "as a testimony to them" (Matthew 8:4). The man spreads the news everywhere, so that Jesus can no longer publicly enter a city (Mark 1:45), and He slips away to the wilderness to pray (Luke 5:16).

**Witnesses:** Matthew 8:2–4 · Mark 1:40–45 · Luke 5:12–16

## 5 · Paralytic Let Down Through the Roof

> And Jesus, seeing their faith, said to the paralyzed man, "Son, your sins are forgiven." — Mark 2:5

**Context.** Back in Capernaum, the house is so full there is no room even near the door. Pharisees and teachers of the Law have come from every village of Galilee and Judea and from Jerusalem, "and the power of the Lord was _present_ for Him to perform healing" (Luke 5:17). Four men carry a paralyzed man, cannot get near, so they remove the roof and let him down on his pallet in front of Jesus. Jesus sees their faith, the faith of the friends, and speaks forgiveness first. The scribes reason in their hearts, "Who can forgive sins except God alone?" (Mark 2:7). Jesus answers them, "so that you may know that the Son of Man has authority on earth to forgive sins," and tells the man, "I say to you, get up, pick up your pallet, and go home" (Mark 2:10–11). He gets up and walks out in front of them all, and they glorify God.

## He Gave Them Power

> Heal _the_ sick, raise _the_ dead, cleanse those with leprosy, cast out demons. Freely you received, freely give. — Matthew 10:8

**Context.** Jesus does not keep healing to Himself. He summons the twelve and gives them "authority over unclean spirits, to cast them out, and to heal every disease and every sickness" (Matthew 10:1). He sends them out in pairs (Mark 6:7), and they are "anointing with oil many sick people and healing them" (Mark 6:13). Then He sends out seventy-two more, who return with joy: "Lord, even the demons are subject to us in Your name!" (Luke 10:17). After the resurrection He says these signs will accompany those who have believed: "they will lay hands on the sick, and they will recover" (Mark 16:18). And before the cross: "the one who believes in Me, the works that I do, he will do also; and greater _works_ than these he will do; because I am going to the Father" (John 14:12). This is what the meditation stands on.
