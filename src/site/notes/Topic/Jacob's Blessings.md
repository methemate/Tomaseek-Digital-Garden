	- **Wordplay on two different roots** — the poem descends in order:

		- The movement: *sounded decisive → still just thinking.* All talk, no action. KJV collapses both to "searchings" and loses the descent.
	- The rhetorical question "Why did you sit between the two sheepfolds?" (לָמָּה יָשַׁבְתָּ) is the sting: Gen 49:4 prophesied it, Judges 5 records it happening.

- God genuinely **raised him up**: "I exalted thee out of the dust, and made thee prince over my people Israel" ([[KJV Tagged + Lexicon/Bible 2/11 - 1 Kings/1 Kings 16#2\|1 Kings 16#2]]). The raising was a gift — not the problem.
